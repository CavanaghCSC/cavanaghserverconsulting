About

Hey! My name is Cavanagh. I run a small project where I review Discord servers and provide suggestions to help them run smoother and grow.

Each week I review around 3–4 servers and give clear recommendations for improving structure, organisation, and community flow.

Note: I focus on server design and management. I do not configure bots.

What I Review

Server Layout

Channel structure and category organisation for clarity.

Role Structure

Improving role hierarchy and permissions.

Community Flow

Making servers easier for new members to navigate.

Growth Setup

Optimising your server layout to support community growth.

How Reviews Work

1. Invite

You invite me to your Discord server so I can analyse the structure.

2. Review

I review channels, roles, categories, and overall organisation.

3. Suggestions

You receive feedback and suggestions to improve your server.
